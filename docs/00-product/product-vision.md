# SERVIQ — Product Vision

## Product

SERVIQ is an app-first on-demand service platform initially focused on
electrical services in India.

## Vision

To make home services transparent, reliable, trackable, and easy to manage
for customers and service professionals.

## Primary Applications

1. SERVIQ Customer — mobile application
2. SERVIQ Partner — mobile application
3. SERVIQ Business — web-based operations dashboard

## Initial Service

Electrical services.

## Core Capabilities

- Customer management
- Service catalogue
- Transparent pricing
- Service booking
- Partner availability
- Partner dispatch
- Real-time job status
- GPS tracking
- Additional work approval
- Digital invoices
- Online payments
- Notifications
- Ratings and reviews
- Operational analytics

## Long-Term Vision

SERVIQ should be capable of expanding beyond electrical services into
multiple home-service categories and supporting multiple service
organizations through a SaaS architecture.

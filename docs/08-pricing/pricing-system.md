# Pricing System

## Purpose

SERVIQ uses transparent pricing so customers can understand the expected service cost before confirming a booking.

## Pricing Models

SERVIQ supports four pricing models.

### Fixed Price

The customer sees a predefined service price.

Example:

Switch Replacement
₹299

The displayed price is the base service price.

### Starting From

The customer sees a minimum starting price.

Example:

Wiring Repair
Starting from ₹499

The final amount can be different when the actual work requires additional materials, units, or effort.

### Per Unit

The service price is multiplied by the required quantity.

Example:

Light Installation
₹199 per light

If the customer requires 3 lights:

₹199 × 3 = ₹597
